11.17–15.17s But Gary Penn remembered terrible handling and endless crashes.

15.17–19.04s Then, Penn recalled, a suspected bug changed the chase.

19.04–23.04s Police tried driving THROUGH players. Subtle customer service.

23.04–26.25s Pip wants control. Timmy prefers chaos.
